**A Certain Magical Index** (*Toaru Majutsu no Index*) 🪄 is a Japanese light novel series turned anime that takes place in Academy City, a futuristic metropolis where roughly eighty percent of the student population develops supernatural "esper" abilities through scientific training programs. The story follows Toma Kamijo, a high school student whose right hand possesses the mysterious power to negate any supernatural ability, whether magical or esper-based, simply by touching it. <br><br>His otherwise ordinary life is upended when he discovers a young nun named Index unconscious in his apartment's balcony, setting off a chain of events that pulls him into the hidden world of sorcerers and secret magical organizations operating outside Academy City. Index herself carries the **Index of Prohibited Books**, a collection of 103,000 grimoires memorized in her mind, making her a target for various factions seeking to exploit this forbidden knowledge. As the series progresses, it weaves together two parallel threads: the scientific side, focused on Academy City's espers and their rigid power rankings, and the occult side, involving churches, magic cabals, and ancient religious conflicts. Kamijo frequently crosses paths with other espers, most notably Mikoto Misaka, a short-tempered electricity-manipulating esper ranked third among the city's most powerful espers, who becomes one of the series' most popular characters. The anime blends ***action, comedy, and supernatural intrigue***, often pitting Kamijo against powerful magicians, rogue scientists, and shadowy organizations with their own agendas. Despite his lack of any power besides his negation ability, Kamijo repeatedly throws himself into dangerous situations to protect the people around him, driven by a strong sense of justice and a refusal to abandon those in need. <br><br>The series has spawned multiple seasons, spin-offs like A Certain Scientific Railgun 🌩️, and various other adaptations, forming a large interconnected franchise. Its worldbuilding combines elements of science fiction and fantasy in a way that distinguishes it from more conventional anime of its genre. The franchise has maintained a dedicated fanbase since the original light novels began publication in the mid-2000s, with the anime adaptations helping popularize its blend of scientific and magical themes to a broader international audience.
